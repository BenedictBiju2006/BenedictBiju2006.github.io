## Overview

MegaSteel needed an online presence that made its product range and business information accessible to prospective customers. I initially built a corporate website, then expanded the project to address a practical maintenance problem: business staff should be able to update the site without understanding React, Git, or deployment workflows.

The objective was to provide a maintainable, business-oriented website without adding a separate database or paid content management subscription.

## What I Built

I developed the website using Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. It includes a responsive landing page, searchable product catalogue, category navigation, product detail pages, company information, and contact functionality.

I subsequently separated editable content from presentation into structured JSON documents for products, categories, pages, and site settings. I built a custom administration dashboard with form-based editors, image uploads, Google OAuth sign-in, and an approved-account access policy.

The CMS uses GitHub as its content store, separating drafts from published content through branches. Staff can save changes, preview drafts, and publish updates through the dashboard, with published commits triggering the existing Vercel deployment workflow.

## Impact

The website provides MegaSteel with a digital catalogue and a central location for business information. The CMS creates a path for non-technical staff to maintain that content independently while retaining Git-backed version history and the existing deployment infrastructure.

The project reflects a shift from building a public-facing website to designing an end-to-end maintainable system around the needs of its operators. No quantified traffic or administrative time savings have been established.
