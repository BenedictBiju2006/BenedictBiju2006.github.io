## Overview

I built MegaSteel's corporate website and expanded it into an administration platform for a steel business in Botswana. The goal was to make both website maintenance and everyday sales operations easier for non-technical staff.

## What I Built

Developed a responsive Next.js, React, and TypeScript website with a searchable product catalogue, product pages, and contact information. Built a GitHub-backed CMS with Google OAuth, image uploads, draft previews, and publishing through Vercel.

Extended the admin dashboard with a server-side Zoho Books integration for customer and product lookup and draft invoice creation. Implemented mixed-unit pricing (quantity × optional length × rate), OAuth token handling, validation, Zoho-issued invoice numbers, and line-item custom fields that preserve original rates and lengths. Invoice editing is under refinement.

## Impact

The platform brings website updates and invoicing into one business-specific interface while keeping Zoho Books as the accounting system. Length-based pricing reduces the need for manual calculations; the invoicing workflow is still being tested.
