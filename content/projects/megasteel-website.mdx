## Overview

I built MegaSteel's corporate website and expanded it into a business management platform for a steel manufacturer and supplier in Botswana.

## What I Built

- Developed a responsive Next.js, React and TypeScript website with a searchable product catalogue and a GitHub-backed CMS, including Google OAuth, image uploads, draft previews and Vercel publishing.
- Integrated Zoho Books for customer and product lookups, invoice creation and editing, sales orders and invoice conversion. Implemented mixed-unit pricing, custom line-item fields, OAuth token handling and server-side validation.
- Built a business dashboard with steel-specific engineering and pricing calculators, exchange rate monitoring for USD, ZAR and BWP, and shortcuts to sales workflows.
- Developed an AI-powered Market Outlook module using the OpenAI Responses API, web search and validated structured outputs to generate source-linked weekly steel market briefings across South Africa, Asia and global markets. Implemented shared server-side caching with weekly invalidation to limit API usage without a dedicated database.

## Impact

The platform consolidates website administration, sales operations and procurement intelligence into a single business-specific interface, while retaining Zoho Books as the accounting system. It reduces manual pricing calculations and provides a central point of access to industry research.
